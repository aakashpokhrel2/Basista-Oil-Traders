# Employee User Manual (कर्मचारी प्रयोगकर्ता पुस्तिका)
**Day-to-Day Operations Guide for Petrol Station Staff**
**Basista Oil Traders (बसिस्ट आयल ट्रेडर्स)**

---

## 1. Getting Started (सुरुवात)

### 1.1 Logging In (लग-इन)
1. Open your web browser and go to: `https://petrol.basistaoil.com.np`
2. Enter your assigned **Username** and **Password**.
3. Click **Sign In (लग-इन गर्नुहोस्)**.

> [!NOTE]
> For security, entering an incorrect password 5 consecutive times will temporarily lock your account for 15 minutes.

### 1.2 Language Switcher (भाषा परिवर्तन)
You can switch between **English** and **नेपाली (Nepali)** at any time by clicking the language dropdown in the top navigation bar. Your preference is automatically saved to your profile.

### 1.3 Dual Calendar (AD र BS मिति)
Dates throughout the system are shown in both **Gregorian (AD)** and **Bikram Sambat (BS)** formats. Nepali Devanagari numerals are supported across all receipts and reports.

## 2. Pump Operator Guide (पम्प अपरेटर पुस्तिका)

### 2.1 Starting a Shift (सिफ्ट सुरु गर्ने)
1. Navigate to **Shifts (सिफ्ट)** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Start New Shift (नयाँ सिफ्ट सुरु गर्नुहोस्)**.
3. Select your assigned Dispenser Pump and enter your **Opening Cash Float (सुरुवाती नगद)**.
4. Record the **Opening Meter Reading** for each nozzle.
5. Click **Open Shift (सिफ्ट सुरु गर्नुहोस्)**.

### 2.2 Ending a Shift & Handover (सिफ्ट समाप्ति र नगद बुझाउने)
1. At the end of duty, go to your active shift.
2. Enter the **Closing Meter Reading** for each nozzle.
   - *Validation*: Closing reading cannot be less than opening reading.
3. Count and enter the physical cash collected (**Closing Cash**).
4. Hand over cash to the Cashier/Manager and submit for **Manager Approval**.

## 3. Cashier Guide (क्यासियर पुस्तिका)

### 3.1 Recording Fuel Sales (इन्धन बिक्री प्रविष्टि)
1. Click **Sales (बिक्री)** -> **New Sale (नयाँ बिक्री)**.
2. Select Customer Type:
   - **Cash (नगद)**: Retail motorist paying cash or digital QR/bank transfer.
   - **Credit (उधारो)**: Registered corporate client or fleet customer.
3. Select Fuel Type (Petrol MS or Diesel HSD) and Dispensing Nozzle.
4. Enter Quantity in Liters or Total Amount in NPR. The system automatically calculates the other.
5. Enter Vehicle Registration Number (गाडी नम्बर) if requested.
6. Click **Print Invoice / रसिद छाप्नुहोस्**.

### 3.2 Collecting Customer Credit Payments (उधारो असुली)
1. Navigate to **Customers (ग्राहक)** -> **Credit Ledger**.
2. Select customer and click **Collect Payment (भुक्तानी लिनुहोस्)**.
3. Enter payment amount, payment method (Cash / Bank Deposit), and reference number.
4. Issue payment receipt. Customer balance updates instantly.

---

## 4. Accountant Guide (लेखापाल पुस्तिका)

### 4.1 Daily Station Expenses (दैनिक खर्च प्रविष्टि)
1. Go to **Expenses (खर्च)** -> **Record Expense**.
2. Select Date, Category (Electricity, Rent, Staff Salary, Generator Fuel, Maintenance, etc.).
3. Enter Amount, Description, and Payment Account (Cash in Drawer / Bank Account).
4. Attach scanned bill or receipt.
5. Submit for ledger posting.

### 4.2 Supplier Purchases & Payments (आपूर्तिकर्ता भुक्तानी)
1. Under **Purchases (खरिद)**, verify incoming fuel tanker invoice from Nepal Oil Corporation (NOC).
2. Enter delivered liters and receiving storage tank.
3. Record bank payments against approved supplier purchase invoices.

### 4.3 Generating Financial Reports (वित्तीय प्रतिवेदन)
1. Navigate to **Reports (प्रतिवेदन)**.
2. Select target date range in Bikram Sambat (BS) or Gregorian (AD).
3. Export **Profit & Loss Statement (नाफा-नोक्सान हिसाब)** as PDF or **Sales Register** as Excel (XLSX).
